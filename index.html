About the Studio

An Independent Studio
with a Global Vision

Dhillon Interactive is an indie game development and software publishing studio founded to build memorable, mechanics-driven experiences that push the boundaries of interactive storytelling.

From medieval strategy epics to multiplayer party games, Dhillon Interactive builds a portfolio for players who crave depth, strategy, and true immersion.

Privacy Policy
Dhillon Interactive — Effective 1 January 2026
Effective: 1 Jan 2026 Dhillon Interactive Haryana, India Version 1.0

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Dhillon Interactive ("we", "us", or "our") — registered at Village Babroli, Block Jatusana, City Kosli, District Rewari, Pincode 123401, Haryana, India — handles information in connection with our mobile and PC games, applications, and related services (the "Services").

By accessing or using our Services you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to the practices described herein.

✅ Short version: We do not collect, store, or sell your personal information directly. Our games and apps are designed to function without requiring account creation or personal details.

2. Information We Collect

Dhillon Interactive does not directly collect personally identifiable information (PII) from users. Specifically:

  • We do not require account registration to play our games.
  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or postal address through our games.
  • We do not collect financial or payment information.
  • We do not collect biometric data or government identification numbers.
  • We do not track your precise real-time GPS location.
  • Any events or alarms you create within the app are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to our servers.

However, certain third-party SDKs integrated into our Services may collect non-personally identifiable technical information automatically. See Section 4 for details.

3. Automatically Collected Technical Data

When you use our Services, certain technical data may be collected automatically:

  • Device information: Device model, OS version, unique device identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID), screen resolution, and hardware specs.
  • Usage data: Session duration, game levels accessed, in-app events, crash reports, and performance metrics.
  • Network information: General IP address region (country/city level only — not precise location), network type.
  • App diagnostics: Error logs, crash data, and ANR reports to help us improve stability.

🔒 This data is collected in aggregate and anonymised form and cannot be used to personally identify you.

4. Third-Party Analytics & Advertising

Our games may incorporate third-party SDKs for analytics and advertising. These operate under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase / Google Play Services: Used to understand player interactions. Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob (if applicable): Our app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob automatically collects your device's Advertising ID and IP address to serve relevant ads. You can reset or opt-out of personalized ads using your Android device's Google settings. Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity Analytics / Unity Ads (if applicable): Used for crash reporting, engagement analytics, and ad delivery. Policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

We select partners who comply with GDPR, CCPA, and COPPA where applicable.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Our games are not directed at children under the age of 13 years (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction).

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
  • If we become aware that a child under 13 has submitted personal data, we will promptly delete it.
  • Games suitable for younger audiences are designed with limited or no data collection and no behavioural advertising.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, contact us immediately at admin@dhillon-interactive.com.

6. How We Use Collected Information

Limited non-personal technical data is used solely for:

  • Maintaining, operating, and improving the performance and stability of our games.
  • Understanding aggregate player behaviour and improving game design.
  • Diagnosing technical issues, crashes, and bugs.
  • Delivering contextually relevant advertisements via third-party ad SDKs.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.
  • Technical data is used for: ... ensuring the accurate triggering of user-set alarms and notifications.

We do not sell, trade, or rent any user data to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

7. Your Privacy Rights

  • Right to Access: Request information about data collected about you.
  • Right to Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to Opt-Out: Opt out of personalised advertising via device settings ("Opt out of Ads Personalisation" on Android).
  • GDPR Rights (EU/EEA): Right to rectification, portability, restriction of processing, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.
  • CCPA Rights (California): Right to know, right to delete, and right to opt-out of sale of personal information (we do not sell personal information).

To exercise any rights contact: admin@dhillon-interactive.com

8. Data Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organisational measures to protect data against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. No electronic transmission or storage method is 100% secure.

9. Third-Party Links

Our games and website may link to third-party services (e.g., Google Play Store). This Privacy Policy applies only to our own Services. We encourage you to review third-party privacy policies independently.

10. Changes to This Policy

When we make material changes, we will:

  • Update the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy.
  • Post the revised policy at dhillon-interactive.com.
  • Provide an in-app notice for significant changes where feasible.

Continued use of our Services after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
